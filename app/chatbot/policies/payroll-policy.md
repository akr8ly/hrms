# Payroll Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-PAY-001: Salary Payment Date

Monthly salary is normally credited on the final working day of each month. Bank-processing delays may affect the time at which funds appear.

## POL-PAY-002: Payslips

Payslips are made available through the approved employee portal after payroll is processed.

## POL-PAY-003: Payroll Corrections

Employees should report payroll discrepancies to HR or Payroll within five working days of receiving the payslip.

## POL-PAY-004: Bank Account Changes

Bank account changes must be submitted through the approved HR process before the monthly payroll cutoff date.

## POL-PAY-005: Statutory Deductions

Applicable tax and statutory deductions are calculated according to current regulations and the declarations submitted by the employee.
