# Information Security Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-SEC-001: Password Protection

Employees must use strong, unique passwords and must not share passwords, authentication codes, or access tokens with another person.

## POL-SEC-002: Device Security

Company devices must use approved security software, screen locking, encryption, and operating-system updates.

## POL-SEC-003: Phishing and Suspicious Activity

Suspicious messages, links, attachments, login prompts, or account activity must be reported to the security team immediately.

## POL-SEC-004: Data Classification

Company and employee information must be handled according to its classification and shared only with authorized recipients.

## POL-SEC-005: Security Incidents

Lost devices, accidental disclosures, unauthorized access, and other suspected security incidents must be reported immediately through the approved incident channel.
