# Code of Conduct Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-CONDUCT-001: Respectful Workplace

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and business partners with dignity and respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## POL-CONDUCT-002: Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose personal, financial, or professional interests that could influence or appear to influence business decisions.

## POL-CONDUCT-003: Gifts and Hospitality

Gifts or hospitality that could improperly influence a business decision must not be offered or accepted. Employees should follow the approved disclosure process.

## POL-CONDUCT-004: Company Property

Company property, systems, funds, and information must be used for authorized business purposes and protected from misuse or loss.

## POL-CONDUCT-005: Reporting Concerns

Employees should report suspected misconduct through their manager, HR, or an approved reporting channel. Good-faith reports must not result in retaliation.
