# Employee Benefits Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-BEN-001: Benefits Eligibility

Benefit eligibility depends on employment type, joining date, location, and the terms of the applicable benefit plan.

## POL-BEN-002: Health Insurance

Eligible employees may enroll in the company health insurance plan during the permitted enrollment period and may add eligible dependants according to plan rules.

## POL-BEN-003: Benefit Changes

Employees should notify HR of eligible life events within the period specified by the applicable benefit plan.

## POL-BEN-004: Wellness Benefits

Eligible wellness expenses may be reimbursed up to the applicable plan limit when supported by valid documents.

## POL-BEN-005: Benefits on Exit

Benefits may end or change when employment ends. HR will provide information about applicable coverage dates and continuation options.
