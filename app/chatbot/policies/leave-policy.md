# Leave Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-LEAVE-001: Casual Leave

Employees receive 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave should be requested at least one working day in advance whenever possible.

## POL-LEAVE-002: Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested when sick leave exceeds two consecutive working days.

## POL-LEAVE-003: Earned Leave

Employees receive 18 days of earned leave per calendar year. Earned leave should normally be requested at least seven calendar days in advance.

## POL-LEAVE-004: Leave Approval

Leave requests must be submitted to the reporting manager. A leave request is confirmed only after the reporting manager approves it.

## POL-LEAVE-005: Leave Balance

Employees should review their available leave balance before submitting a request. Leave taken beyond the available balance may be treated as unpaid leave unless HR approves an exception.
