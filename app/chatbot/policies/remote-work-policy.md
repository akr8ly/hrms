# Remote Work Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-REMOTE-001: Remote Work Eligibility

Remote work may be available when the employee's role, performance, and business requirements support it. Approval from the reporting manager is required.

## POL-REMOTE-002: Work Location

Employees must work from an approved location with a reliable internet connection and an environment suitable for confidential work.

## POL-REMOTE-003: Availability

Remote employees must remain reachable during agreed working hours through approved communication channels.

## POL-REMOTE-004: Equipment

Company equipment must be used responsibly and returned when requested. Loss, damage, or technical problems should be reported promptly.

## POL-REMOTE-005: Data Protection

Confidential information must not be viewed or discussed where unauthorized people can access it. Approved security controls and company systems must be used.
