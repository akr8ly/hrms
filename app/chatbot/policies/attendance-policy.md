# Attendance Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-ATT-001: Working Hours

The standard workday is eight working hours, excluding the meal break. Employees should follow the schedule assigned to their office or team.

## POL-ATT-002: Attendance Recording

Employees must record attendance through the approved attendance system at the beginning and end of the workday.

## POL-ATT-003: Late Arrival

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their reporting manager as early as possible. Repeated late arrivals may be reviewed by the reporting manager and HR.

## POL-ATT-004: Remote Work Attendance

Employees working remotely must remain available during agreed working hours and record attendance using the same approved system.

## POL-ATT-005: Attendance Correction

Missing or incorrect attendance entries should be submitted for correction within three working days and require reporting-manager approval.
