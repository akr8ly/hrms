# Travel Policy

> Sample development policy. Replace this content with approved HR policy before production use.

## POL-TRAVEL-001: Travel Approval

Business travel requires approval from the reporting manager before tickets or accommodation are booked.

## POL-TRAVEL-002: Approved Booking Method

Employees should use the company-approved booking process for business travel unless an exception is approved.

## POL-TRAVEL-003: Travel Expenses

Reasonable business travel expenses may be reimbursed when supported by valid receipts and submitted through the approved expense process.

## POL-TRAVEL-004: Expense Submission

Travel expense claims should be submitted within ten working days after the trip is completed.

## POL-TRAVEL-005: Personal Expenses

Personal purchases, upgrades, fines, and expenses unrelated to approved business travel are not reimbursable.
